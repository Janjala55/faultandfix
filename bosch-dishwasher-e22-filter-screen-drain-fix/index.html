Bosch Dishwasher E22 Error Code: Sump Filter Cleanout & Flow Restriction Fix

By Fault and Fix Diagnostic Engineering Team
Updated: Nov 19, 2026
OEM Verified
Fault Code: E22 (Filter Mesh Blockage / Flow Restricted) Reviewed by Master Appliance Technician
What It Means: Sump Filter Screen Clogged or Sump Flow Restricted (Drain pump experiences high hydraulic suction resistance due to grease, calcium, or food debris blinding the micro-mesh filter)
Most Likely Root Cause: A thick, invisible coating of grease, animal fats, and detergent binders clogging the microscopic pores of the stainless steel cylindrical mesh filter (00645038).
Repair Difficulty: Beginner DIY
Estimated Time: 15–20 minutes
Required Tools: Old toothbrush or stiff nylon brush, degreasing dish soap, hot water, sponge
Applicable Models: Bosch 300, 500, 800 Series, SilencePlus, Ascenta, Benchmark series
Immediate Safe Action / 60-Second Quick Check

Unscrew the cylindrical filter in bottom of tub. Hold under sink tap: if water pools on the mesh instead of flowing straight through, the micro-mesh is blinded with fat.

1. Symptoms & Operational Failure Signs

When code E22 (Filter Mesh Blockage / Flow Restricted) triggers, the appliance controller detects abnormal electrical feedback or physical boundary limits out of range. Check for these real-world failure symptoms:

  • Dishwasher displays 'E22' during intermediate drain phases or at end of cycle.
  • Water drains very slowly, leaving cloudy greasy standing water in bottom sump well.
  • Dishes have a gritty film or white greasy haze after drying.
  • Drain pump produces a strained, high-pitched whining noise.

2. The 30-Second Hard Reset Sequence

Clear temporary software glitches, parasitic capacitive voltage, and transient relay lockouts with this manufacturer capacitive discharge sequence:

  1. Press and hold Start/Reset button for 3 seconds until display reads '0:01'.
  2. Close door to initiate 1-minute forced drain purge.
  3. Clean the filter cylinders thoroughly under scalding hot water with soap.
  4. Reinstall filter and run an empty Hot wash cycle with 2 cups of white vinegar.

3. Entering Hidden Factory Diagnostic Mode

Execute this authorized technician button cadence to retrieve stored historical error logs and force individual component actuation tests:

  1. With power off, press and hold '>' and '<' buttons while pressing Power.
  2. Enter Customer Service Diagnostic Program 'P0'.
  3. Press Start to cycle to Step S01 (Forced Drain Test).
  4. Verify drain pump empties sump in under 20 seconds with clean filter.

4. Multimeter Resistance (Ω) Specifications & Harness Pinouts

Disconnect electrical power before probing harness terminals. Set your digital multimeter to the lowest Ohms (Ω) scale and verify the circuit integrity against OEM specifications:

5. Step-by-Step Mechanical Tear-Down & Repair

Follow these 5 OEM service manual steps to access, inspect, and replace the failing component safely:

  1. Remove Lower Dish Rack: Roll bottom dish rack out of tub to access sump basin.
  2. Unlock Filter Assembly: Rotate the central cylindrical filter counter-clockwise 1/4 turn and lift cylinder straight up out of sump.
  3. Remove Flat Metal Screen: Lift the flat stamped stainless steel mesh plate out of the bottom tub floor.
  4. Scrub with Degreaser: Wash both filter parts in sink using scalding hot water, Dawn dish soap, and a stiff toothbrush. Scrub until water passes through micro-mesh instantly like air.
  5. Inspect White Impeller Cover: Check that the small white plastic drain pump impeller cover (00611322) is clicked tightly down into place, then reinstall filter.

6. Frequently Asked Questions

Why did error E22 trigger when my filter looked clean to the eye?

Micro-pores in the stainless filter screen become coated with a clear, microscopic film of cooked animal fats and rinse aid residue. It looks visually clean, but water cannot pass through it under pump suction.

How often should I clean the Bosch dishwasher filter to prevent E22?

Under normal daily use, remove and rinse the cylindrical filter once every 3 to 4 weeks. If washing greasy roasting pans or heavily soiled plates, clean it every 2 weeks.
