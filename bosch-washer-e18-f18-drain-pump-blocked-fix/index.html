Bosch Washing Machine E18 / F18 Error Code: Drain Pump Cleanout & Obstruction Guide

By Fault and Fix Diagnostic Engineering Team
Updated: Nov 24, 2026
OEM Verified
Fault Code: E18 / F18 (Drain Pump Blocked / Drain Timeout) Reviewed by Master Appliance Technician
What It Means: Drain Timeout Fault (Washer fails to evacuate wash water within 90 seconds, halting cycle before spin phase)
Most Likely Root Cause: Coins, bobby pins, or small socks jammed in the drain pump filter chamber or sheared drain pump impeller blades (00145777).
Repair Difficulty: Beginner DIY
Estimated Time: 20–30 minutes
Required Tools: Coin trap tray, towels, channel lock pliers, flashlight
Applicable Models: Bosch 300, 500, 800 Series, Serie 4, Serie 6 front load series
Immediate Safe Action / 60-Second Quick Check

Open lower right access door on front of washer. Place towel down and prepare to drain water through auxiliary black hose.

1. Symptoms & Operational Failure Signs

When code E18 / F18 (Drain Pump Blocked / Drain Timeout) triggers, the appliance controller detects abnormal electrical feedback or physical boundary limits out of range. Check for these real-world failure symptoms:

  • Water remains trapped in drum and door refuses to unlock.
  • Display flashes 'E18' or 'F18' with continuous alarm beeps.
  • Drain pump makes a strained humming or grinding sound.
  • Spin cycle will not run with water detected in tub.

2. The 30-Second Hard Reset Sequence

Clear temporary software glitches, parasitic capacitive voltage, and transient relay lockouts with this manufacturer capacitive discharge sequence:

  1. Unplug washer from 120V outlet for 2 minutes.
  2. Drain standing water via lower emergency drain hose into a bowl.
  3. Unscrew round drain filter counter-clockwise and remove debris.
  4. Reinstall filter tightly and run Rinse/Spin cycle.

3. Entering Hidden Factory Diagnostic Mode

Execute this authorized technician button cadence to retrieve stored historical error logs and force individual component actuation tests:

  1. Power off washer. Turn knob to 6 o'clock (Spin).
  2. Press and hold 'Spin Speed' while turning knob to 7 o'clock.
  3. Release button: display enters Customer Service Mode.
  4. Advance to P1 (Pump Test) and press Start to test pump rotation.

4. Multimeter Resistance (Ω) Specifications & Harness Pinouts

Disconnect electrical power before probing harness terminals. Set your digital multimeter to the lowest Ohms (Ω) scale and verify the circuit integrity against OEM specifications:

Component & Harness Terminals Expected OEM Reading (Normal) Failure Threshold (Replace Component)
Drain Pump Motor Winding 14.0 Ω to 18.0 Ω (Normal stator coil) Infinite (OL) burned open coil
Pressure Switch Analogue Output 0.5V to 2.5V DC proportional to water level 0.0V DC (Sensor line fault)
Door Latch Solenoid 90 Ω to 130 Ω (Lock coil resistance) Infinite (OL) open circuit

5. Step-by-Step Mechanical Tear-Down & Repair

Follow these 5 OEM service manual steps to access, inspect, and replace the failing component safely:

  1. Disconnect Electrical Supply: Unplug washer power cord.
  2. Drain Residual Water: Pull out small black rubber drain hose on bottom right, remove stopper plug, and empty water into flat tray.
  3. Unthread Coin Filter: Unscrew large black circular filter cap counter-clockwise.
  4. Clean Impeller Well: Reach into pump chamber with finger: rotate the 4-blade impeller wheel—it should turn with notchy magnetic resistance. Remove hair and coins.
  5. Reseat & Test: Screw filter cap tightly until vertical alignment mark matches, test Rinse cycle.

6. Frequently Asked Questions

Why did my Bosch washer door stay locked when E18 triggered?

For safety, Bosch washers will never unlock the door if the pressure sensor detects water above the lower door rim. You must drain the water via the lower drain tube to allow the safety lock to release.

What happens if the drain filter cap won't turn?

A coin or bra underwire can wedge between the filter threads and the housing. Use channel lock pliers gently on the cap handle with moderate leverage, wiggling back and forth until the debris dislodges.
