LG Refrigerator Ice Maker Stuck in Harvest: Ejector Arm Motor AEQ73110205 Diagnostic

By Fault and Fix Diagnostic Engineering Team
Updated: Nov 27, 2026
OEM Verified
Fault Code: Ice Ejector Fingers Stuck in Ice Mold / No Ice Reviewed by Master Appliance Technician
What It Means: Ice Harvest Motor Stalled Mid-Rotation (Ice ejector arm stops at 6 o'clock position in frozen ice mold because mold heater failed to release ice or drive gear stripped)
Most Likely Root Cause: Stripped plastic reduction gear inside ice maker drive head (AEQ73110205 / AEQ72909602) or open mold release heating element.
Repair Difficulty: Intermediate DIY
Estimated Time: 30–45 minutes
Required Tools: Phillips screwdriver, hair dryer, digital multimeter, small mirror
Applicable Models: LG LFXS26973, LRFDS3016, LFXC24796, Kenmore Elite 795 series
Immediate Safe Action / 60-Second Quick Check

Locate small test button on underside of ice maker housing. Press and hold for 3 seconds: if motor hums without arm moving, internal gears are stripped.

1. Symptoms & Operational Failure Signs

When code Ice Ejector Fingers Stuck in Ice Mold / No Ice triggers, the appliance controller detects abnormal electrical feedback or physical boundary limits out of range. Check for these real-world failure symptoms:

  • Ice ejector sweep fingers are stuck pointing straight down into ice cube mold.
  • Ice maker produces zero ice and ice bucket remains empty.
  • Rhythmic clicking or skipping gear sound heard from freezer door every hour.
  • Front dispenser display may show error code 'Er IS' (Ice Sensor/Motor Error).

2. The 30-Second Hard Reset Sequence

Clear temporary software glitches, parasitic capacitive voltage, and transient relay lockouts with this manufacturer capacitive discharge sequence:

  1. Turn off ice maker power switch on unit or dispenser display.
  2. Unplug refrigerator power cord for 5 minutes.
  3. Restore power and press test button on underside of ice maker for 3 seconds.
  4. Observe whether harvest arm completes a full 360-degree rotation.

3. Entering Hidden Factory Diagnostic Mode

Execute this authorized technician button cadence to retrieve stored historical error logs and force individual component actuation tests:

  1. Press and hold test button on underside of ice maker module for 3 seconds.
  2. Ice harvest cycle will initiate: arm rotates forward to eject ice.
  3. Water valve will energize for 6 seconds at end of cycle to refill mold.
  4. If arm stalls in ice mold, mold heater is not energizing.

4. Multimeter Resistance (Ω) Specifications & Harness Pinouts

Disconnect electrical power before probing harness terminals. Set your digital multimeter to the lowest Ohms (Ω) scale and verify the circuit integrity against OEM specifications:

Component & Harness Terminals Expected OEM Reading (Normal) Failure Threshold (Replace Component)
Ice Mold Heater Terminals 80 Ω to 120 Ω (Normal mold heating loop) Infinite (OL) burned open mold heater
Harvest Motor Drive Winding 2.0 kΩ to 3.5 kΩ (Synchronous gear motor) 0.0 Ω short or Infinite OL
Water Valve Ice Solenoid 180 Ω to 240 Ω (Normal solenoid coil) Infinite (OL) burned valve coil

5. Step-by-Step Mechanical Tear-Down & Repair

Follow these 5 OEM service manual steps to access, inspect, and replace the failing component safely:

  1. Disconnect Electrical Supply: Unplug refrigerator power cord.
  2. Remove Ice Storage Bin: Pull ice bin forward and remove from freezer door.
  3. Unscrew Ice Maker Module: Remove two Phillips screws securing ice maker assembly to door frame.
  4. Disconnect Wire Harness: Unplug multi-pin electrical harness connector.
  5. Install OEM Replacement: Mount new OEM ice maker assembly (AEQ73110205), reconnect harness, secure screws, replace ice bucket, and press test button to prime water fill.

6. Frequently Asked Questions

Why did the ejector fingers get stuck in the ice cubes?

Before the ejector fingers push ice cubes out of the mold, an internal mold heater warms the tray to 35°F for 30 seconds to release the ice bond. If this heater fails, the motor attempts to push against solid frozen ice, stripping the plastic gears.

Can I replace just the stripped gears?

LG does not sell individual internal gears for the modular ice maker. The complete factory replacement ice maker module (AEQ73110205) is required.
