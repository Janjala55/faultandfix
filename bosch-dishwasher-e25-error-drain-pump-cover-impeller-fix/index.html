Bosch Dishwasher E25 Error Code: Pump Impeller Cover Snap-In & Cleanout Guide

By Fault and Fix Diagnostic Engineering Team
Updated: Nov 16, 2026
OEM Verified
Fault Code: E25 (or E24 / E25 Alternating) Reviewed by Master Appliance Technician
What It Means: Drain Pump Blockage or Missing Impeller Cover (Drain pump cannot create negative suction pressure because white plastic impeller cover is unclipped or impeller is mechanically jammed)
Most Likely Root Cause: The small white plastic pump impeller cover (00611322) was not snapped fully into place after cleaning, or a broken glass shard/lemon seed is wedged between impeller vanes.
Repair Difficulty: Beginner DIY
Estimated Time: 15–20 minutes
Required Tools: Sponge, Torx T20 screwdriver, small teaspoon, protective work gloves
Applicable Models: Bosch 300, 500, 800 Series, SilencePlus, Ascenta, Thermador, Gaggenau
Immediate Safe Action / 60-Second Quick Check

Bail standing water out of sump with a sponge. Reach into bottom sump well and check if the small curved white plastic impeller cover clicks firmly into position.

1. Symptoms & Operational Failure Signs

When code E25 (or E24 / E25 Alternating) triggers, the appliance controller detects abnormal electrical feedback or physical boundary limits out of range. Check for these real-world failure symptoms:

  • Dishwasher cycle halts 3 to 4 minutes before end with display flashing 'E25' or 'E24'.
  • Standing dirty grey water remains pooled in bottom basin above the micro-filter.
  • Water tap icon light illuminates red on front control panel.
  • Drain pump makes a faint humming or clicking sound but fails to expel water into sink.

2. The 30-Second Hard Reset Sequence

Clear temporary software glitches, parasitic capacitive voltage, and transient relay lockouts with this manufacturer capacitive discharge sequence:

  1. Open door and press and hold Start/Reset button firmly for 3 to 5 seconds until display reads '0:01' or '0:00'.
  2. Close dishwasher door immediately to allow drain pump to execute a 1-minute forced drain sequence.
  3. Wait for cycle completion chime, open door, and confirm sump water has drained completely.
  4. Power off and on to clear error memory.

3. Entering Hidden Factory Diagnostic Mode

Execute this authorized technician button cadence to retrieve stored historical error logs and force individual component actuation tests:

  1. Power dishwasher off. Press and hold '<' and '>' program keys simultaneously, then press Power.
  2. Hold buttons until display shows 'P0' or 'P1' (Customer Service Test Program).
  3. Press Start to cycle through automated actuator tests: Step S01 forces drain pump activation.
  4. Listen for smooth high-speed drain pump rotation and inspect clear discharge hose.

4. Multimeter Resistance (Ω) Specifications & Harness Pinouts

Disconnect electrical power before probing harness terminals. Set your digital multimeter to the lowest Ohms (Ω) scale and verify the circuit integrity against OEM specifications:

5. Step-by-Step Mechanical Tear-Down & Repair

Follow these 5 OEM service manual steps to access, inspect, and replace the failing component safely:

  1. Remove Sump Filter Cylinders: Turn central micro-filter cylinder counter-clockwise 1/4 turn and lift stainless filter mesh out of sump.
  2. Sponge Out Residual Water: Use an absorbent sponge to soak up all standing water from the bottom circular sump pit until bone dry.
  3. Pry Off White Pump Cover: Using the handle of a small teaspoon or Torx T20, unhook the catch tab on the small curved white plastic impeller cover (left side of sump) and pull cover forward and out.
  4. Clear Impeller Vanes: Inspect the exposed drain impeller. Spin the rubber/plastic impeller with your finger—it should rotate with a distinctive magnetic 'notchy' resistance. Remove any glass shards, toothpicks, or olive pits.
  5. Snap Cover Back In Until Audible CLICK: Reinsert white cover hook first, press down firmly until you hear a sharp, loud 'CLICK'. (Without this click, the pump cannot create suction and code E25 will trigger instantly!). Reinstall filter.

6. Frequently Asked Questions

Why does error E25 keep coming back after I cleaned the filter?

In 85% of cases, the white plastic pump cover was replaced but not pushed down hard enough to engage the locking barb. If that cover is loose by even 1 millimeter, water recirculates in the well instead of pushing past the check valve.

What is the difference between Bosch code E24 and E25?

Error E24 indicates a general drain restriction (often a clogged sink air gap or disposal knockout plug), while E25 specifically indicates that the drain pump impeller is jammed or the pump cover is unclipped.
