LG Front Load Washer Leaking From Front Door: Door Boot Gasket 4986ER0004F Diagnostic

By Fault and Fix Diagnostic Engineering Team
Updated: Dec 04, 2026
OEM Verified
Fault Code: Water Dripping From Bottom Front Door During Wash Reviewed by Master Appliance Technician
What It Means: Door Boot Bellow Gasket Puncture or Tear (Grey rubber door gasket has developed a rip, tear, or mold rot in lower fold, allowing wash water to pour down front cabinet)
Most Likely Root Cause: Sharp clothing zippers, bra underwires, or coins puncturing the lower drainage fold of the rubber door boot gasket (4986ER0004F / MDS47123608).
Repair Difficulty: Intermediate DIY
Estimated Time: 45–60 minutes
Required Tools: Spring expansion tool (or needle-nose pliers), Phillips screwdriver, dish soap solution
Applicable Models: LG WM3400, WM3900, WM4000, WM9000, Signature front load series
Immediate Safe Action / 60-Second Quick Check

Peel back the lowest fold of the grey rubber door gasket with your fingers. Look for small punctures, tears, or a missing drain hole grommet.

1. Symptoms & Operational Failure Signs

When code Water Dripping From Bottom Front Door During Wash triggers, the appliance controller detects abnormal electrical feedback or physical boundary limits out of range. Check for these real-world failure symptoms:

  • Puddle of water leaks onto floor beneath front left corner of door during wash agitation.
  • Water visibly streams down front enamel panel from under glass door frame.
  • Torn rubber flap or puncture hole found in lowest gasket fold.
  • Severe black mold stains embedded inside rubber gasket crevices.

2. The 30-Second Hard Reset Sequence

Clear temporary software glitches, parasitic capacitive voltage, and transient relay lockouts with this manufacturer capacitive discharge sequence:

  1. Unplug washer power cord from 120V outlet.
  2. Wipe standing water from floor and lower front cabinet.
  3. Inspect door glass rim: clean lint buildup from glass sealing surface.
  4. If gasket has visible tear or rip, replace rubber boot before running wash loads.

3. Entering Hidden Factory Diagnostic Mode

Execute this authorized technician button cadence to retrieve stored historical error logs and force individual component actuation tests:

  1. Power unit on, hold 'Spin Speed' + 'Soil Level' for 3 seconds to enter Quick Test Mode.
  2. Press Start 2 times to test high-level water fill.
  3. Observe lower door gasket area with flashlight to pinpoint water leak origin.
  4. Press Power to cancel test and drain water.

4. Multimeter Resistance (Ω) Specifications & Harness Pinouts

Disconnect electrical power before probing harness terminals. Set your digital multimeter to the lowest Ohms (Ω) scale and verify the circuit integrity against OEM specifications:

Component & Harness Terminals Expected OEM Reading (Normal) Failure Threshold (Replace Component)
Door Lock Solenoid Resistance 80 Ω to 125 Ω (Normal lock coil) Infinite (OL) open circuit
Drain Pump Motor Winding 12.0 Ω to 18.0 Ω (Normal stator coil) 0.0 Ω short or Infinite OL
Water Inlet Valve Hot/Cold Coils 1.0 kΩ to 1.5 kΩ (Nominal valve coil) Infinite (OL) burned coil

5. Step-by-Step Mechanical Tear-Down & Repair

Follow these 5 OEM service manual steps to access, inspect, and replace the failing component safely:

  1. Disconnect Electrical Supply: Unplug washer power cord.
  2. Remove Outer Spring Clamp: Locate spring on outer perimeter wire clamp around door opening. Stretch spring with pliers to remove wire.
  3. Peel Gasket from Front Panel: Peel front rubber lip off metal cabinet flange.
  4. Remove Front Cabinet Panel: Unscrew door lock screws, top panel, and lower kickplate screws to remove main front metal panel.
  5. Install OEM Replacement Boot: Release inner spring clamp holding boot to outer tub. Lubricate new OEM gasket (4986ER0004F) with liquid dish soap, align bottom drain holes, secure inner and outer spring clamps, and test for leaks.

6. Frequently Asked Questions

Why did my door boot tear in the first place?

The #1 culprit is a stray bra underwire that poked through the basket holes, or a sharp metallic jacket zipper that caught the rubber lip during a high-speed spin cycle.

How can I keep the new door gasket from smelling like mold?

Always leave the washer door cracked 2 inches open when not in use to allow moisture to evaporate, and wipe out the lowest rubber fold with a dry microfiber cloth after the day's final load.
