Maytag Washer F0 E2 Error Code: Excess Suds Routine & Pressure Switch Cleanout

By Fault and Fix Diagnostic Engineering Team
Updated: Nov 18, 2026
OEM Verified
Fault Code: F0 E2 / Suds Lock Reviewed by Master Appliance Technician
What It Means: Excess Foam or Aerated Water Detection (Pressure transducer detects high air pressure head caused by detergent foam cushioning the wash basket)
Most Likely Root Cause: Overdosing non-HE laundry detergent or soap scum clogging the lower tub air dome pressure tube, falsely signaling high water level.
Repair Difficulty: Beginner DIY
Estimated Time: 30–45 minutes
Required Tools: 1/4" nut driver, syringe or vinyl tubing, hot water, white vinegar
Applicable Models: Maytag Bravos, MVWB765, MVWB835, MVWC465 series
Immediate Safe Action / 60-Second Quick Check

Check tub interior for excessive foam bubbles. Pour 1 cup vegetable oil or 2 cups white vinegar directly into tub to instantly collapse the soap suds.

1. Symptoms & Operational Failure Signs

When code F0 E2 / Suds Lock triggers, the appliance controller detects abnormal electrical feedback or physical boundary limits out of range. Check for these real-world failure symptoms:

  • Washer cycle halts during rinse or spin cycle with display flashing 'F0 E2' or 'SUD'.
  • Drain pump runs continuously for over 10 minutes trying to pump aerated foam.
  • Water fails to pump out because drain pump impeller cavitates in soap lather.
  • Clothes come out soapy with white chemical streaks.

2. The 30-Second Hard Reset Sequence

Clear temporary software glitches, parasitic capacitive voltage, and transient relay lockouts with this manufacturer capacitive discharge sequence:

  1. Allow washer to sit undisturbed for 20 minutes to allow detergent foam to collapse naturally.
  2. Press Power/Cancel button once to clear the active alarm chime.
  3. Select 'Rinse & Spin' with Cold Water and press Start without adding any extra soap.
  4. Run an empty cycle with hot water to purge residual soap deposits from outer tub.

3. Entering Hidden Factory Diagnostic Mode

Execute this authorized technician button cadence to retrieve stored historical error logs and force individual component actuation tests:

  1. Enter technician diagnostic mode: Turn selector knob counter-clockwise 1 click.
  2. Turn knob 3 clicks Right, 1 click Left, 1 click Right within 5 seconds.
  3. Turn selector knob until 'Spin' LED lights up (Manual Drain Test).
  4. Press Start to force drain pump to operate at maximum voltage.

5. Step-by-Step Mechanical Tear-Down & Repair

Follow these 5 OEM service manual steps to access, inspect, and replace the failing component safely:

  1. Disconnect Power: Unplug washer from 120V electrical outlet.
  2. Access Air Dome Tube: Remove rear upper sheet metal cover panel on washer console to expose clear plastic pressure hose.
  3. Disconnect Pressure Tube: Pull clear vinyl hose off the pressure sensor nozzle on the main control board.
  4. Blow Out Obstructions: Blow forcefully into the clear hose: you should hear bubbles or air rushing freely into bottom of outer tub. If resistance is felt, flush with warm water.
  5. Reattach Hose Clamps: Push hose tightly back onto sensor nipple, ensure zero kinks, and reinstall rear cover.

6. Frequently Asked Questions

Why does F0 E2 keep happening even when I don't add soap?

Liquid detergent and fabric softener build up a thick gummy layer (scrud) on the hidden outside wall of the inner drum. During agitation, this residual soap re-lathers, triggering F0 E2 on plain water cycles.

Can I use regular non-HE detergent if I use less?

No. Modern high-efficiency washers use very little water and high agitation speeds. Non-HE soap contains high-foaming surfactants that cause pump cavitation and error F0 E2.
