Whirlpool Dryer AF Error Code: Restricted Airflow & Venting Diagnostic

By Fault and Fix Diagnostic Engineering Team
Updated: Nov 18, 2026
Reviewed by Marcus Vance (Senior Appliance Specialist)
5 min read
Verified OEM Fault Diagnostic AF
Verified against Whirlpool OEM Engineering Service Specs
Official Fault Meaning: Air Flow Restriction: Exhaust Venting Clogged or External Hood Damper Stuck
Most Likely Root Cause: Heavy lint accumulation in 4-inch wall duct, crushed foil transition duct, stuck bird guard louver outdoors, or clogged lint chute.
Repair Difficulty: Intermediate DIY
Estimated Time: 30 mins
Required Tools: Vent Cleaning Brush Kit, Drill, 1/4-inch Nut Driver, Multimeter
Applicable Models: WED8500DC, WED7500GW, WED5605MW, WGD8500DC, WED9620HC, MEDB835DW
Immediate Safe Action / 60-Second Quick Check

Disconnect flex duct from wall exhaust outlet and check for solid lint blockage.

1. Symptoms & Operational Failure Signs

When code AF triggers, the central microcontroller detects anomalous feedback or sensor voltage out of range. Check for these symptoms:

  • 'AF' flashing on display; clothes take 2 to 3 full cycles to dry completely
  • Top and sides of dryer cabinet feel burning hot to the touch during operation
  • Moisture condensation drips inside dryer door glass during cycle
  • High-limit thermostat cycles heater off prematurely every 30 seconds

2. The 30-Second Hard Reset Sequence

Clear temporary software lockouts, residual filter capacitor voltage, and transient logic anomalies with this factory capacitive drain routine:

  1. Press the CANCEL button to clear the AF code.
  2. Unplug the dryer and pull it away from the wall.
  3. Detach the flexible metal transition duct and clean out accumulated lint clumps.
  4. Go outside and verify the exterior exhaust damper flap opens freely when the dryer runs.

3. Entering Hidden Factory Diagnostic Mode

Execute this technician keypad/dial cadence to force direct component test routines, cycle individual actuators, and read stored historical fault codes:

  1. How Whirlpool detects AF: The control board calculates the rate of temperature rise and fall across the exhaust thermistor.
  2. If the heater shuts off but the exhaust temperature drops very slowly, low airflow is confirmed.
  3. Run a test cycle with the exhaust duct completely disconnected from the wall into the room (for electric dryers only!).
  4. If AF does not recur with vent disconnected, the restriction is 100% inside your household wall ducting.

4. Multimeter Resistance (Ω) Specifications & Harness Pinouts

Disconnect electrical power before probing harness terminals. Set your digital multimeter to the lowest Ohms (Ω) scale and test the critical circuit path:

Component & Harness Terminals Expected OEM Reading (Normal) Failure Threshold (Replace Component)
Exhaust Thermistor at 77°F (25°C)
Thermistor Terminals (Harness Disconnected)		 9.5 kΩ to 10.5 kΩ (Nominal 10.0 kΩ at 77°F) Infinite OL or value deviating > 15%
High-Limit Safety Thermostat
Thermostat Terminals on Heater Duct		 0.0 Ω (Closed Circuit Continuity at Room Temp) Infinite OL (Tripped open due to vent overheating)
Electric Heating Element Coil
Heater Terminals (Harness Off)		 7.8 Ω to 12.5 Ω (Nominal 10.2 Ω for 240V 5400W) Infinite OL (Burned open coil filament)

5. Step-by-Step Mechanical Tear-Down & Repair

Follow these 5 OEM service manual steps to access, inspect, and replace the failing component safely:

  1. Power Off: Unplug the dryer electrical cord from the wall outlet.
  2. Duct Disconnect: Loosen worm-drive clamp and pull flexible aluminum duct off the dryer blower collar.
  3. Rotary Brush Cleaning: Feed a rotary lint brush attached to a cordless drill into the wall ducting from outside to inside.
  4. Lint Chute Vacuuming: Remove lint filter screen. Insert narrow vacuum crevice attachment deep into the blower housing duct.
  5. Reassembly & Re-Check: Reattach rigid metal ductwork, secure with foil tape (never use screws in ducts!), and test.

6. Frequently Asked Questions

Will the dryer continue to run if the AF code is displayed?

Yes, on most models the dryer will complete the cycle with reduced heater duty, but it will flash AF at the end to warn you of severe fire hazards and excessive energy consumption.

Can I use flexible plastic or vinyl ductwork?

Never! Flexible plastic or vinyl ducting is a severe fire code violation. It easily crushes, traps lint in ridges, and melts instantly in a lint fire. Always use rigid or semi-rigid aluminum metal ducting.
