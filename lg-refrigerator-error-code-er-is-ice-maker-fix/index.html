LG Refrigerator Er IS Error Code: In-Door Ice Maker Motor & Sensor Repair

By Fault and Fix Diagnostic Engineering Team
Updated: Nov 16, 2026
Reviewed by Marcus Vance (Senior Appliance Specialist)
5 min read
Verified OEM Fault Diagnostic Er IS
Verified against LG OEM Engineering Service Specs
Official Fault Meaning: Ice Maker Sensor Open/Short or Motor Harvest Rotation Timeout
Most Likely Root Cause: Twist ice tray jammed by oversized ice bridge, failed drive motor microswitch, or defective ice mold thermistor.
Repair Difficulty: Intermediate DIY
Estimated Time: 30 mins
Required Tools: Phillips Screwdriver, Digital Multimeter, 1/4-inch Nut Driver, Towel
Applicable Models: LFXS26973S, LFXS28968S, LRFXC2416S, LMXS28626S, LFXC24726S, LFXS30796S
Immediate Safe Action / 60-Second Quick Check

Inspect ice tray for trapped ice cubes. Press and hold the small ice maker reset button underneath the motor housing for 3 seconds.

1. Symptoms & Operational Failure Signs

When code Er IS triggers, the central microcontroller detects anomalous feedback or sensor voltage out of range. Check for these symptoms:

  • 'Er IS' or '1S' displayed on control panel; ice maker completely stops harvesting
  • Ice maker motor continuously hums or clicks repeatedly without dumping ice
  • Ice cubes frozen into one giant solid block in the in-door ice storage bucket
  • Twist ice tray stuck at a 45-degree angle in mid-ejection cycle

2. The 30-Second Hard Reset Sequence

Clear temporary software lockouts, residual filter capacitor voltage, and transient logic anomalies with this factory capacitive drain routine:

  1. Open the left-hand refrigerator door to access the slim in-door SpacePlus ice maker compartment.
  2. Remove the ice bucket by lifting up and pulling straight out.
  3. Locate the small square spring-loaded test button on the underside or bottom edge of the ice maker head.
  4. Press and hold the button for 3 full seconds. The motor should chime and begin an automatic 360-degree harvest rotation.
  5. If the tray completes its twist and returns home, the code is cleared.

3. Entering Hidden Factory Diagnostic Mode

Execute this technician keypad/dial cadence to force direct component test routines, cycle individual actuators, and read stored historical fault codes:

  1. Ensure ice maker power switch on the housing is turned ON.
  2. Enter Smart Diagnosis via LG ThinQ app or refrigerator button sequence ('Freezer' + 'Ice Plus' for 3 seconds).
  3. Listen for drive motor gear engagement. A loud stripping or clicking indicates stripped nylon reduction gears.
  4. Verify ice maker mold thermistor resistance at main PCB connector CON8.

4. Multimeter Resistance (Ω) Specifications & Harness Pinouts

Disconnect electrical power before probing harness terminals. Set your digital multimeter to the lowest Ohms (Ω) scale and test the critical circuit path:

Ice Mold Thermistor at 32°F (0°C)

5. Step-by-Step Mechanical Tear-Down & Repair

Follow these 5 OEM service manual steps to access, inspect, and replace the failing component safely:

  1. Safety First: Unplug refrigerator and remove the ice bucket and top wire shelf.
  2. Compartment Cover: Remove the two Phillips screws securing the ice maker assembly cover to the inner door liner.
  3. Harness Disconnect: Unclip the multi-pin electrical harness leading from the upper door hinge.
  4. Unit Removal: Depress the plastic retaining latch and slide the ice maker assembly forward and out of the door.
  5. Replacement: Install new OEM LG ice maker assembly (e.g. AEQ73110205 / AEQ73110210), snap harness tight, and press the reset button to verify cycle.

6. Frequently Asked Questions

Can low water pressure cause the Er IS error?

Indirectly, yes. If low water pressure under-fills the tray, thin ice slabs can fracture during twisting and lodge into the mechanical gears, causing the motor to jam and throw the Er IS timeout code.

Does Er IS mean I have to replace the whole ice maker assembly?

In 85% of cases, yes. The motor, harvest gear train, and mold thermistor are integrated into a sealed sub-assembly. Replacing the complete OEM modular assembly is the most reliable long-term fix.
