Samsung Dishwasher 9E Error Code: Low Water Level Sensor Diagnostic

By Fault and Fix Diagnostic Engineering Team
Updated: Nov 10, 2026
Reviewed by Marcus Vance (Senior Appliance Specialist)
5 min read
Verified OEM Fault Diagnostic 9E
Verified against Samsung OEM Engineering Service Specs
Official Fault Meaning: Low Water Level / Pressure Sensor Fault (Control board fails to detect expected water column pressure signal during or after fill stage)
Most Likely Root Cause: Sediment, grease, or calcium buildup blocking the sump pressure switch air chamber, or defective pressure transducer (DD96-00305A).
Repair Difficulty: Intermediate DIY
Estimated Time: 25–35 minutes
Required Tools: Phillips screwdriver, digital multimeter, pliers, small syringe or wire to clear hose
Applicable Models: Samsung DW80M9960, DW80K7050, DW80R9950, DW80J3020 series
Immediate Safe Action / 60-Second Quick Check

Pour 2 quarts of hot water and 1 cup of white vinegar into the dishwasher sump and let soak for 20 minutes to dissolve mineral scale.

1. Symptoms & Operational Failure Signs

When code 9E triggers, the central microcontroller detects anomalous feedback or sensor voltage out of range. Check for these symptoms:

  • Dishwasher fills partially with water then halts and drains immediately
  • Display flashes '9E' within 3 to 5 minutes of starting any wash cycle
  • Circulation pump fails to engage because pressure switch does not confirm water presence
  • Machine repeats fill and drain cycle twice before shutting down completely

2. The 30-Second Hard Reset Sequence

Clear temporary software lockouts, residual filter capacitor voltage, and transient logic anomalies with this factory capacitive drain routine:

  1. Press Power to turn unit off. Switch off circuit breaker for 60 seconds.
  2. Open door, remove bottom rack, and take out coarse stainless sump filter.
  3. Inspect sump well for thick layer of fat or calcified detergent sludge.
  4. Restore breaker power, initiate cycle, and listen for pressure switch trip.

3. Entering Hidden Factory Diagnostic Mode

Execute this technician keypad/dial cadence to force direct component test routines, cycle individual actuators, and read stored historical fault codes:

  1. Access technician diagnostic mode: Press 'Normal' + 'Heavy' + 'Power'.
  2. Cycle to Step 02 (Pressure Switch State Test).
  3. Display will read '0' (Empty / No Pressure) or '1' (Water Detected / Full).
  4. Fill tub manually with a bucket of water; verify display toggles from '0' to '1'.

4. Multimeter Resistance (Ω) Specifications & Harness Pinouts

Disconnect electrical power before probing harness terminals. Set your digital multimeter to the lowest Ohms (Ω) scale and test the critical circuit path:

5. Step-by-Step Mechanical Tear-Down & Repair

Follow these 5 OEM service manual steps to access, inspect, and replace the failing component safely:

  1. Toe-Kick Removal: Switch off circuit breaker. Remove lower toe-kick screws and pull metal access panel away.
  2. Sensor Identification: Locate water level pressure sensor mounted to side of plastic sump basin or lower left chassis.
  3. Pressure Tube Detachment: Use pliers to release spring clamp and pull small rubber pressure tube off sensor port.
  4. Air Chamber Cleanout: Use a warm-water squeeze bottle or syringe to flush out the plastic air chamber on the sump where grease collects.
  5. Sensor Replacement: If air tube is clear but switch tests open under water pressure, replace pressure sensor (DD96-00305A), reclamp tube, and test.

6. Frequently Asked Questions

How does grease cause error 9E?

Food fats and hard water minerals solidify inside the narrow plastic air bell chamber at the bottom of the sump. When water enters, air cannot compress inside the chamber, preventing the pressure sensor diaphragm from registering the water column.

Can running citric acid fix error 9E?

Yes! If the sensor itself is not burned out, running a heavy cycle with 4 ounces of citric acid crystals or hot white vinegar dissolves the calcium and soap scum blocking the pressure port, clearing error 9E without part replacement.
