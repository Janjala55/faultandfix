GE Washer Reset Procedure & Motor Inverter Flash Codes: 6-Lid Open Reset & Diagnostic Mode

By admin
Updated: Oct 13, 2026
Reviewed by Marcus Vance (Senior Appliance Specialist)
4 min read
Verified Fault Diagnostic Motor Inverter Flashes / E22
Verified by GE Technical Service Guide 31-9219
Official Fault Meaning: Motor Drive Microcontroller Standby Lockout / Mode Shifter Fault
Most Likely Cause: Power surge or motor slip triggered inverter lockout, failed mode shifter actuator coil (98 Ω), or defective lid lock switch.
Repair Difficulty: Beginner–Intermediate DIY
Estimated Time: 10–25 minutes
Required Tools: Digital multimeter, 1/4" nut driver, putty knife
Applicable Models: GE GTW220, GTW330, GTW460, GTW680, GTW840, Profile series
Immediate Safe Action / Quick Reset

The GE 6-Lid Open Reset: Unplug washer for 60 seconds. Reconnect power. Within 12 seconds of plugging in, raise and close the washer lid completely 6 times in rapid succession. The motor inverter LED resets from error flash to steady standby pulse.

When a GE top-load washing machine (including Hydrowave, GTW, and Profile series) fills with water but refuses to agitate or spin, homeowners frequently believe the motor or main transmission is destroyed. In reality, modern GE washers isolate the high-power drive electronics inside an intelligent Motor Inverter Module mounted directly onto the electric drive motor beneath the tub. If an unbalanced load, lid cycle anomaly, or voltage surge occurs, the inverter enters a protective software lockout.

Step 1: The Official GE “6-Lid Raise” Motor Reset Sequence

This factory software reset procedure is documented in official GE service technical bulletins and clears motor lockout without tools:

1

Power Down the Washer

Unplug the washing machine power cord from the 120V wall outlet (or trip the dedicated circuit breaker in your electrical service panel).

2

Wait 60 Seconds for Inverter Discharge

Leave the washer unplugged for a minimum of 60 full seconds. This allows the high-voltage electrolytic capacitors inside the motor inverter board to bleed down to 0 volts.

3

Execute the 6-Lid Cycle Within 12 Seconds

Plug the power cord back into the electrical outlet. Within 12 seconds of restoring power, you must completely open and close the washing machine lid 6 times in steady succession. Ensure each closure triggers the physical lid latch switch firmly.

4

Verify Motor Reset

Select any cycle (e.g. Drain & Spin or Casuals) and press START. If the motor engages and spins, the software lockout has been cleared successfully.

Step 2: Decoding the Green Motor Inverter Diagnostic LED Flashes

If the 6-lid reset does not restore operation, look underneath the washer. GE drive motors have a small optical viewing port on top of the aluminum motor inverter cover through which a green diagnostic LED pulses.

Green LED Flash Pattern Official Diagnostic Status Required Action / Diagnostic Meaning
1 Flash per Second (Steady 1s ON / 1s OFF) Normal Standby Inverter is healthy and waiting for run command from console. Problem is lid lock or console board.
Fast Blinking (Continuous Rapid Pulse) Motor Running Normally Motor is receiving drive commands and actively rotating.
4 Flashes Mode Shifter / Clutch Fault Mode shifter coil failed or clutch spring did not engage agitate/spin transition. Test shifter coil (98 Ω).
5 Flashes Motor Thermal Overload Motor internal windings exceeded 135°C thermal limit. Allow 45 minutes to cool; check tub bearing binding.
10 Flashes Lid Switch Fault Motor attempted 4 consecutive starts without seeing the lid switch open between cycles. Replace lid lock #WH01X27954.
Completely Dark (No Light) No 120V Power or Blown Inverter Fuse Check 120V AC supply at motor harness pins 1 & 2. If voltage present, inverter board has failed.

Step 3: Multimeter Test on GE Mode Shifter Actuator Coil (98 Ω)

When the inverter flashes 4 times, the mode shifter electric solenoid coil (mounted alongside the transmission shaft) has failed to switch the mechanical splines between agitation and spin mode.

  • Unplug the washer and tilt it rearward on solid wooden blocks.
  • Locate the cylindrical black mode shifter assembly attached to the transmission casting.
  • Disconnect the 2-pin electrical connector from the mode shifter coil.
  • Set your multimeter to Resistance (Ω) and measure across the two coil terminal pins:
    • Normal OEM Spec: 98 Ω (± 5% / 93 Ω to 103 Ω) at room temperature.
    • Fault: If the meter reads Infinite (O.L.) or under 10 Ω, replace the mode shifter assembly.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why did the 6-lid reset fail the first time I tried it?

Timing is strict: all 6 lid cycles must be completed within 12 seconds of plugging the unit in. If you hesitate past the 12-second window, the inverter timer times out. Repeat the process with steady, full strokes.

Can a faulty lid lock prevent the 6-lid reset from registering?

Yes. The inverter detects the lid opening via the lid switch electrical contacts. If the lid switch microswitch contacts are worn or burned out, the inverter will never receive the 6 closure pulses, remaining in error state (10 flashes).
