Samsung Range C-30 Error Code: Upper Cooling Fan Motor DG96-00110E Diagnostic

By Fault and Fix Diagnostic Engineering Team
Updated: Nov 22, 2026
OEM Verified
Fault Code: C-30 (Cooling Fan Motor Speed Fault) Reviewed by Master Appliance Technician
What It Means: Upper Cooling Motor Rotation Loss (Oven control board detects upper cross-flow cooling fan motor RPM dropping below 800 RPM during Bake or Broil)
Most Likely Root Cause: Seized sleeve bearings in upper cooling blower motor (DG96-00110E) or failed magnetic Hall speed sensor on fan motor stator.
Repair Difficulty: Intermediate DIY
Estimated Time: 40–50 minutes
Required Tools: 1/4" nut driver, Phillips screwdriver, digital multimeter
Applicable Models: Samsung NE59J7850, NE58K9850, FE710, NX58H5600 Slide-in & Freestanding series
Immediate Safe Action / 60-Second Quick Check

Turn off oven. Listen at the horizontal vent slot between cooktop and door: if zero airflow is felt when oven starts preheating, cooling fan is stalled.

1. Symptoms & Operational Failure Signs

When code C-30 (Cooling Fan Motor Speed Fault) triggers, the appliance controller detects abnormal electrical feedback or physical boundary limits out of range. Check for these real-world failure symptoms:

  • Oven display chimes and flashes 'C-30' within 3 minutes of starting Bake.
  • Heating elements immediately shut off and oven cancels active cycle.
  • Control console becomes unusually hot to touch above oven door.
  • Loud screeching or grinding noise from top rear of range prior to error.

2. The 30-Second Hard Reset Sequence

Clear temporary software glitches, parasitic capacitive voltage, and transient relay lockouts with this manufacturer capacitive discharge sequence:

  1. Turn off range 240V/120V circuit breaker in service panel for 5 minutes.
  2. Allow oven control area to cool down completely.
  3. Restore breaker power and attempt Quick Bake at 350°F.
  4. If C-30 returns immediately, fan motor replacement is required.

3. Entering Hidden Factory Diagnostic Mode

Execute this authorized technician button cadence to retrieve stored historical error logs and force individual component actuation tests:

  1. Enter technician test mode: Press 'Bake' and 'Broil' simultaneously for 3 seconds.
  2. Display enters diagnostic menu. Select Fan Motor Test (FAn).
  3. Control commands cooling fan to high speed (approx. 1,800 RPM).
  4. Display shows real-time RPM feedback: if 0 RPM, sensor or motor is dead.

5. Step-by-Step Mechanical Tear-Down & Repair

Follow these 5 OEM service manual steps to access, inspect, and replace the failing component safely:

  1. Disconnect 240V Power: Shut off dual-pole range circuit breakers in electrical panel.
  2. Pull Range Forward: Slide range away from wall to access rear sheet metal.
  3. Remove Upper Rear Cover: Unscrew 1/4" hex screws holding top rear galvanized cover panel.
  4. Locate Cross-Flow Cooling Fan: Locate the long cylindrical squirrel-cage blower motor mounted horizontally across top rear frame.
  5. Install Replacement Blower: Disconnect wiring plug, remove mounting screws, install new OEM cooling fan motor assembly (DG96-00110E / DG31-00005A), and reassemble.

6. Frequently Asked Questions

What is the purpose of the cooling fan motor on Samsung ranges?

The cross-flow cooling fan pulls cool room air across the delicate microprocessors and relays inside the electronic display console, exhausting warm air through the door vents to prevent board overheating.

Why won't Samsung ovens bake if the cooling fan fails?

For fire safety, Samsung firmware locks out all bake and broil relays instantly if cooling airflow is lost. Without active cooling, touch glass temperatures could exceed safety limits and melt internal wiring.
