Whirlpool Washer Error Code F5 E1 (Lid Lock Failure): 6-Click Reset & Latch Test

By admin
Updated: Oct 13, 2026
Reviewed by Dan Miller (Master Appliance Tech)
3 min read
Verified Fault Diagnostic F5 E1
Verified by Whirlpool Service Bulletin W10838613
Official Fault Meaning: Lid Lock Switch Assembly Failure / Lock Solenoid Inability to Verify Closure
Most Likely Cause: Cracked plastic strike bezel, bent lock latch actuator, loose wiring harness J15 connector, or burned solenoid coil.
Repair Difficulty: Beginner to Intermediate DIY
Estimated Time: 20–30 minutes
Required Tools: Putty knife (to pop console spring clips), 1/4" nut driver, digital multimeter, Phillips screwdriver
Applicable Models: Whirlpool Cabrio, Maytag Bravos, Commercial Technology MCT (WTW7300DW, MVWX655DW, WTW8500DC)
Immediate Safe Action / Quick Reset

Perform the 6-Click Reset: In Standby mode, lift and drop the lid firmly 6 times in 12 seconds. If clothes are trapped, reach under the console and slide the manual release arm rearward.

Error code F5 E1 on Whirlpool and Maytag washers signals a Lid Lock Assembly Fault. When you press START, the control board energizes the internal lid lock solenoid to drive a locking bolt into the lid strike. If the internal micro-switch or magnetic reed switch fails to confirm that the bolt is fully seated after 3 rapid attempts (click-click-click), the washer aborts the cycle and refuses to fill.

A locked lid with clothes trapped inside is a frequent emergency. Below is the verified technician protocol for unlocking the lid manually and clearing the error.

Emergency: How to Unlock the Lid Manually (Clothes Trapped)

⚠️ Electrical Isolation

Unplug the washer power cord before sliding any tool under the top console to avoid electric shock from the live harness terminals.

  1. Unplug the washer from the wall outlet.
  2. Slide a thin putty knife approximately 6.5 inches from the left and right front corners under the lid seam to release the metal top-panel retaining clips.
  3. Gently hinge the top panel backward.
  4. Locate the black lid lock body attached beneath the top panel rim.
  5. On the underside of the switch body, reach for the small plastic manual release slider tab and slide it rearward (toward the back of the washer).
  6. The latch bolt will retract immediately, allowing you to lift the lid and retrieve laundry.

Step 1: The 6-Click Console Reset Trick

Often, a temporary mechanical misalignment trips the control board’s safety lockout. To force the micro-controller to re-interrogate the switch:

  • Unplug the machine for 2 minutes.
  • Plug the cord back in.
  • While the console is dark in Standby mode, lift and drop the lid firmly 6 times within 12 seconds.
  • Press START.

Step 2: Testing the Lid Lock with a Multimeter

Disconnect harness connector J15 from the main electronic control board (or test directly at the 4-pin lid switch harness plug).

Terminal Pins Component Tested Expected Resistance
Pins 3 to 4 (White to Blue) Lid Lock Solenoid Coil 85 Ω to 125 Ω (Normal)
Pins 1 to 2 (Yellow to White) Lid Closed Switch (Lid Down) 0.0 Ω (Continuity)
Pins 1 to 2 (Yellow to White) Lid Open Switch (Lid Raised) Infinite (O.L.)

If the solenoid resistance reads 0 Ohms or Infinite, the lock solenoid is blown and the assembly must be replaced.

OEM Replacement Part Information

OEM Genuine Part

Whirlpool / Maytag Lid Lock Switch Assembly

OEM Part Number: W10838613 (Replaces W10404050, W10619006, W10837741)

Compatibility: Whirlpool Cabrio WTW7300, WTW8000, Maytag Bravos MVWB750, Commercial Technology MCT series.

Installation Time: 15 minutes using a putty knife and 1/4″ nut driver.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does the washer click 3 times before displaying F5 E1?

The control board attempts to energize the solenoid 3 consecutive times to push the lock bolt. If the strike is misaligned or the internal sensor contacts are worn, it aborts after the 3rd attempt to prevent solenoid overheating.

Can I bypass the lid lock switch with a jumper wire?

No. Modern Whirlpool machines use microprocessors that monitor dynamic pulse feedback between the lock solenoid and the reed switch. A static jumper wire will trigger a persistent F5 E3 error (Lid Switch Open While Locked) and halt all cycles permanently.
