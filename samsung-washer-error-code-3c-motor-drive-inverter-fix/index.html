Samsung Washer 3C Error Code: Motor Drive Inverter & Stator Hall Sensor Fix

By Fault and Fix Diagnostic Engineering Team
Updated: Nov 8, 2026
Reviewed by Marcus Vance (Senior Appliance Specialist)
5 min read
Verified OEM Fault Diagnostic 3C
Verified against Samsung OEM Engineering Service Specs
Official Fault Meaning: Motor Drive Inverter / Over-Current Fault (Inverter detects motor stall, open stator phase, or Hall sensor signal dropout)
Most Likely Root Cause: Failed Rotor Position Sensor (Hall RPS), loose stator terminal harness plug, or overloaded drum bearings.
Repair Difficulty: Intermediate DIY
Estimated Time: 35–50 minutes
Required Tools: 10mm socket, 17mm socket & ratchet, digital multimeter, Phillips screwdriver
Applicable Models: Samsung WF45R6100, WF42H5200, WA50M7450, WF50A8600 series
Immediate Safe Action / 60-Second Quick Check

Reduce load size, remove bound laundry wedged between inner basket and front bellow, and power cycle.

1. Symptoms & Operational Failure Signs

When code 3C triggers, the central microcontroller detects anomalous feedback or sensor voltage out of range. Check for these symptoms:

  • Washer drum twitches slightly 2 or 3 times then halts without tumbling
  • Humming sound from rear motor assembly followed by error 3C or 3E chime
  • Washer fills with water but refuses to agitate or spin
  • Drum feels stiff or difficult to turn smoothly by hand

2. The 30-Second Hard Reset Sequence

Clear temporary software lockouts, residual filter capacitor voltage, and transient logic anomalies with this factory capacitive drain routine:

  1. Unplug washer electrical power cord from wall outlet.
  2. Press and hold Power and Start buttons simultaneously for 10 seconds to dissipate inverter capacitor charge.
  3. Spin the stainless drum by hand 10 revolutions; verify quiet, resistance-free bearing rotation.
  4. Restore power and run empty 'Quick Wash' cycle to monitor motor initiation.

3. Entering Hidden Factory Diagnostic Mode

Execute this technician keypad/dial cadence to force direct component test routines, cycle individual actuators, and read stored historical fault codes:

  1. Turn power on. Press 'Delay End' and 'Soil Level' simultaneously for 3 seconds.
  2. Rotate cycle knob until motor test code 'Mot' or 'tst' appears on display.
  3. Press Start/Pause to energize low-speed agitation (50 RPM clockwise, then counter-clockwise).
  4. Observe drum action; if motor halts instantly with buzzing, inverter overcurrent protection has tripped.

4. Multimeter Resistance (Ω) Specifications & Harness Pinouts

Disconnect electrical power before probing harness terminals. Set your digital multimeter to the lowest Ohms (Ω) scale and test the critical circuit path:

5. Step-by-Step Mechanical Tear-Down & Repair

Follow these 5 OEM service manual steps to access, inspect, and replace the failing component safely:

  1. Rear Panel Access: Disconnect 120V power and water hoses. Slide washer away from wall and remove 4 to 6 rear metal access panel hex screws.
  2. Rotor Bolt Removal: Hold outer rotor bell housing steady. Use a 17mm socket with breaker bar to loosen center rotor retaining bolt (counter-clockwise).
  3. Rotor Extraction: Carefully slide magnetic rotor bell straight off splined drive shaft. Keep fingers clear of powerful permanent neodymium magnets.
  4. Stator & Sensor Removal: Disconnect wiring harness plug. Remove six 10mm bolts securing stator coil ring to bearing housing. Unclip Hall RPS sensor from stator frame.
  5. Part Replacement & Torque: Clip new Hall sensor (DC31-00098A) onto stator, bolt stator to 8.5–11 ft-lbs, reinstall rotor with threadlocker, and torque center bolt to 40 ft-lbs.

6. Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between 3C, 3E, and 3C1 on Samsung washers?

All represent motor drive errors. 3C / 3E indicate motor stall or open windings, 3C1 indicates motor overcurrent, and 3C2 indicates weak drive inverter signal. The Hall sensor is the common root cause in 80% of cases.

Can a bad door latch cause a 3C error?

Yes. If the door lock microswitch contacts pit or drop voltage under motor load, the main PCB loses confirmed latch continuity during drum ramp-up, cutting inverter drive and logging 3C.
