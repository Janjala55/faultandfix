Samsung Refrigerator Water Under Crispers: Defrost Drain Tube Freeze-Up Fix

By Fault and Fix Diagnostic Engineering Team
Updated: Nov 21, 2026
OEM Verified
Fault Code: Water Under Crisper Drawers / Ice in Bottom of Fridge Reviewed by Master Appliance Technician
What It Means: Defrost Condensate Drain Hole Frozen (Defrost water cannot flow into compressor evaporation pan, backing up and freezing into a solid ice sheet under pantry drawers)
Most Likely Root Cause: Factory aluminum drain heater clip is too short to conduct heat into the drain funnel, allowing slush to freeze and plug the rubber drain duckbill valve.
Repair Difficulty: Intermediate DIY
Estimated Time: 45–60 minutes
Required Tools: Phillips screwdriver, hot water squeeze bottle or steamer, towels, work light
Applicable Models: Samsung RF28, RF26, RH22, RB21, French Door & Bottom Mount series
Immediate Safe Action / 60-Second Quick Check

Remove lower deli drawer. If standing water or clear sheet ice covers the bottom plastic floor, your defrost drain line is frozen solid.

1. Symptoms & Operational Failure Signs

When code Water Under Crisper Drawers / Ice in Bottom of Fridge triggers, the appliance controller detects abnormal electrical feedback or physical boundary limits out of range. Check for these real-world failure symptoms:

  • Puddle of water collects under vegetable bins every 24 to 48 hours.
  • Water leaks out of bottom door and ruins wood kitchen floors.
  • Evaporator fan produces a loud chattering noise hitting ice.
  • Frost or ice crust forms across the lower back wall vents.

2. The 30-Second Hard Reset Sequence

Clear temporary software glitches, parasitic capacitive voltage, and transient relay lockouts with this manufacturer capacitive discharge sequence:

  1. Unplug refrigerator from 120V outlet for 10 minutes.
  2. Soak up all water from bottom shelf with towels.
  3. Flush drain hole with boiling water until it drains freely.
  4. Plug cord back in and monitor drain pan behind refrigerator.

3. Entering Hidden Factory Diagnostic Mode

Execute this authorized technician button cadence to retrieve stored historical error logs and force individual component actuation tests:

  1. Press 'Energy Saver' and 'Fridge' buttons simultaneously for 8 seconds.
  2. Display flashes 'Fd' (Forced Defrost).
  3. Press 'Fridge' pad repeatedly until 'rd' (Refrigerator Defrost) shows.
  4. Defrost heater will energize for 30 minutes to melt back-wall ice.

Disconnect electrical power before probing harness terminals. Set your digital multimeter to the lowest Ohms (Ω) scale and verify the circuit integrity against OEM specifications:

5. Step-by-Step Mechanical Tear-Down & Repair

Follow these 5 OEM service manual steps to access, inspect, and replace the failing component safely:

  1. Disconnect Power & Empty Shelves: Unplug refrigerator and remove crisper drawers and lower glass shelves.
  2. Remove Rear Cover Panel: Remove 4 Phillips screws holding the white plastic interior evaporator cover panel.
  3. Disconnect Harness: Gently pry cover forward and unplug fan and temperature sensor wire connectors.
  4. Melt Drain Hole Ice: Inject boiling water from a squeeze bottle into the metal drain hole trough until water rushes through into rear drain pan.
  5. Install Extended Y-Clip: Remove short factory clip from defrost heater rod. Install OEM extended stainless drain heater clip (DA61-06796A) extending 1 inch down into drain hole, then reinstall panel.

6. Frequently Asked Questions

Why does the drain hole freeze up even when the heater works?

The aluminum defrost heater loop ends 1 inch above the drain hole. When cold air circulates, the first drops of defrost water freeze in the unheated drain funnel. Installing the extended stainless clip carries radiant heat deep into the hole.

Do I need to check the rubber duckbill valves in the back?

Yes! Behind the lower rear sheet metal panel, two black rubber duckbill valves cap the drain spouts. Squeeze them to remove sticky algae and snip the rubber tip slightly so water drains freely.
