LG Washer tCL Error Code: Tub Clean Cycle Reminder & Maintenance Reset

By Fault and Fix Diagnostic Engineering Team
Updated: Nov 17, 2026
OEM Verified
Fault Code: tCL (Tub Clean Reminder) Reviewed by Master Appliance Technician
What It Means: Tub Clean Maintenance Notification (Washer has completed 30 wash cycles and requires a sanitizing cleanout cycle to remove bio-film and scale)
Most Likely Root Cause: Routine firmware reminder counter reaching 30 cycles; not an electrical component malfunction.
Repair Difficulty: Beginner DIY
Estimated Time: 5 minutes (setup) + 90 min (cycle)
Required Tools: Washing machine cleaner tablet (e.g. Affresh) or 1 cup liquid chlorine bleach, microfiber cloth
Applicable Models: LG WM3400, WM3900, WM4000, WT7300, WT7800 front & top load series
Immediate Safe Action / 60-Second Quick Check

If you need to do urgent laundry, simply turn the cycle knob to Normal: tCL will disappear for that cycle until next idle state.

1. Symptoms & Operational Failure Signs

When code tCL (Tub Clean Reminder) triggers, the appliance controller detects abnormal electrical feedback or physical boundary limits out of range. Check for these real-world failure symptoms:

  • Digital display flashes 'tCL' when washer is powered on instead of cycle time.
  • Faint musty or mildew odor emanating from door rubber gasket and tub interior.
  • Detergent drawer has built-up fabric softener residue or gummy detergent scale.
  • Water draining from washer contains dark soap flakes or mineral deposits.

2. The 30-Second Hard Reset Sequence

Clear temporary software glitches, parasitic capacitive voltage, and transient relay lockouts with this manufacturer capacitive discharge sequence:

  1. Empty the washer tub completely of all clothes and laundry items.
  2. Add 1 tablet of oxygenated washing machine cleaner directly into empty drum (or 1 cup bleach in dispenser).
  3. Turn Power on, rotate cycle dial to 'Tub Clean' (or press and hold 'Tub Clean' button for 3 seconds).
  4. Press Start/Pause and allow the 90-minute high-heat sanitizing cycle to complete fully without interruption.

3. Entering Hidden Factory Diagnostic Mode

Execute this authorized technician button cadence to retrieve stored historical error logs and force individual component actuation tests:

  1. Press Power to turn machine on.
  2. Press and hold 'Spin Speed' and 'Soil Level' simultaneously for 3 seconds.
  3. Display flashes all LEDs then shows firmware software revision (e.g. '04').
  4. Turn cycle knob to review total cumulative lifetime wash cycles recorded by EEPROM.

4. Multimeter Resistance (Ω) Specifications & Harness Pinouts

Disconnect electrical power before probing harness terminals. Set your digital multimeter to the lowest Ohms (Ω) scale and verify the circuit integrity against OEM specifications:

5. Step-by-Step Mechanical Tear-Down & Repair

Follow these 5 OEM service manual steps to access, inspect, and replace the failing component safely:

  1. Run Full Tub Clean: Execute Tub Clean cycle using manufacturer-approved cleaner to dissolve internal biofilm behind stainless basket.
  2. Clean Rubber Door Bellow: Open front door, peel back grey rubber door boot gasket folds, and wipe away lint/mold with 50/50 bleach and water solution.
  3. Service Lower Coin Trap Filter: Open lower left access door, drain residual water through black drain tube into bowl, and twist filter counter-clockwise.
  4. Rinse Trap Screen: Remove debris, coins, and lint hairballs from filter chamber under warm running water. Inspect drain pump impeller for free rotation.
  5. Reinstall & Wipe Dispenser: Reinsert coin trap filter tightly, remove detergent dispenser drawer, rinse siphon tubes, and reinstall.

6. Frequently Asked Questions

Can I bypass tCL without running Tub Clean?

Yes. Turn the program selector dial to any standard wash cycle (such as Normal or Heavy Duty). The tCL message will clear, allowing your wash load to run normally. However, tCL will reappear after every load until a Tub Clean cycle is completed.

Why does my LG washer smell like mildew even after running Tub Clean?

Mildew spores thrive in the dark, damp crevices of the front door rubber boot gasket. After every load, leave the washer door cracked 2 inches open to allow air circulation and wipe out the lowest gasket fold dry.
