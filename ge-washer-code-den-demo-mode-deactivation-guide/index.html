GE Washer dEn Code: Showroom Demo Mode Deactivation Guide

By Fault and Fix Diagnostic Engineering Team
Updated: Nov 20, 2026
Reviewed by Marcus Vance (Senior Appliance Specialist)
5 min read
Verified OEM Fault Diagnostic dEn
Verified against GE Appliances OEM Engineering Service Specs
Official Fault Meaning: Showroom / Demo Mode Active: Display Operates but Valves, Motor, and Heater are Disabled
Most Likely Root Cause: Accidental long-press of keypad button combination during cleaning, child tampering, or washer delivered directly from retail floor display.
Repair Difficulty: Intermediate DIY
Estimated Time: 10 mins
Required Tools: None Required (Keypad Cadence)
Applicable Models: GFW850SSNWW, GFW650SSNWW, GFW550SSNWW, GTW720BSNWS, GTW685BSLWS, GFD85ESSNWW
Immediate Safe Action / 60-Second Quick Check

Do not disassemble washer. Execute the model-specific 3-button keypad release cadence.

1. Symptoms & Operational Failure Signs

When code dEn triggers, the central microcontroller detects anomalous feedback or sensor voltage out of range. Check for these symptoms:

  • 'dEn' or 'DEMO' flashes on the digital display screen upon pressing START
  • Console lights cycle through simulated wash stages in 60 seconds without taking in water
  • Motor does not spin and water inlet valves never click open
  • Door locks and unlocks in rapid succession during demonstration cycle

2. The 30-Second Hard Reset Sequence

Clear temporary software lockouts, residual filter capacitor voltage, and transient logic anomalies with this factory capacitive drain routine:

  1. Wake washer display by pressing any button.
  2. Press and hold 'EXTRA RINSE' and 'PREWASH' simultaneously for 5 full seconds.
  3. Display will chime 3 times and show 'dEn OFF' or revert to normal cycle time (e.g., '54 MIN').
  4. If model has no Prewash button, press and hold 'SOIL LEVEL' and 'TEMP' simultaneously for 5 seconds.

3. Entering Hidden Factory Diagnostic Mode

Execute this technician keypad/dial cadence to force direct component test routines, cycle individual actuators, and read stored historical fault codes:

  1. If button sequences fail, perform complete capacitive power discharge.
  2. Unplug power cord from wall outlet for 15 minutes.
  3. Press and hold POWER button for 10 seconds while unplugged to drain internal PCB capacitors.
  4. Plug washer back in and verify normal cycle start.

4. Multimeter Resistance (Ω) Specifications & Harness Pinouts

Disconnect electrical power before probing harness terminals. Set your digital multimeter to the lowest Ohms (Ω) scale and test the critical circuit path:

5. Step-by-Step Mechanical Tear-Down & Repair

Follow these 5 OEM service manual steps to access, inspect, and replace the failing component safely:

  1. Keypad Cadence: Power on washer. Press and hold Temp + Delay Wash (or Soil + Extra Rinse) for 5 seconds.
  2. Display Confirmation: Watch display. When 'dEn' changes to standard wash cycle time, release buttons.
  3. Power Cycle: Power machine off, wait 10 seconds, and power back on. Verify normal cycle display.
  4. Test Cycle: Select Express Wash and press START. Verify cold water inlet valve energizes with audible snap.
  5. Control Board Replacement: In rare cases where EEPROM is locked permanently in demo mode, replace main board (part WH22X37434).

6. Frequently Asked Questions

Why did my brand-new GE washer arrive in dEn mode?

Retail appliance showrooms enable demo mode so shoppers can press buttons and see illuminated LED light shows without the washer running water or spinning on the sales floor. Occasionally, floor models or warehouse units are shipped without deactivating this mode.

Can I damage my washer by running it in demo mode?

No, demo mode strictly disables all high-voltage loads (water valves, drive motor, drain pump, and heater). It poses zero risk of damage, but prevents you from doing laundry until deactivated.
