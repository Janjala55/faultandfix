Frigidaire Refrigerator H1 / HI High Temp Alarm: Dirty Coils & Evaporator Fan Diagnostic

By Fault and Fix Diagnostic Engineering Team
Updated: Nov 28, 2026
OEM Verified
Fault Code: H1 / HI (High Temperature Alarm Flashing) Reviewed by Master Appliance Technician
What It Means: Cabinet Temperature Above Safe Threshold (Freezer temperature has exceeded 26°F or refrigerator temperature has exceeded 55°F for longer than 60 minutes)
Most Likely Root Cause: Heavy lint carpet insulating condenser coils beneath refrigerator, failed evaporator fan motor (241509402), or door left cracked open.
Repair Difficulty: Beginner DIY
Estimated Time: 20–30 minutes
Required Tools: Condenser coil cleaning brush, vacuum cleaner, flashlight
Applicable Models: Frigidaire Gallery, Professional, FGHS2631, FGHD2368, Side-by-Side and French Door series
Immediate Safe Action / 60-Second Quick Check

Press 'Alarm Reset' or 'Mute' pad on control panel to silence beeper. Vacuum thick dust from black condenser coils underneath the front kickplate.

1. Symptoms & Operational Failure Signs

When code H1 / HI (High Temperature Alarm Flashing) triggers, the appliance controller detects abnormal electrical feedback or physical boundary limits out of range. Check for these real-world failure symptoms:

  • Display flashes 'H1' or 'HI' accompanied by loud beeping every 30 seconds.
  • Ice cream in freezer is soft or melting into soup.
  • Compressor runs constantly with a strained, high-pitched buzz.
  • Exterior metal cabinet sides between doors feel burning hot.

2. The 30-Second Hard Reset Sequence

Clear temporary software glitches, parasitic capacitive voltage, and transient relay lockouts with this manufacturer capacitive discharge sequence:

  1. Press Alarm Reset button to silence alarm buzzer.
  2. Ensure doors are closed completely with magnetic gaskets sealing airtight.
  3. Vacuum lint from front bottom condenser coils.
  4. Allow refrigerator 4 to 6 hours to pull down below 26°F; H1 will extinguish automatically.

3. Entering Hidden Factory Diagnostic Mode

Execute this authorized technician button cadence to retrieve stored historical error logs and force individual component actuation tests:

  1. Press and hold 'Freezer Warmer' and 'Fresh Food Colder' for 5 seconds.
  2. Unit enters Service Diagnostic Mode.
  3. Step to Test 15 to verify evaporator fan motor runs at maximum speed.
  4. Step to Test 04 to verify compressor is running under load.

4. Multimeter Resistance (Ω) Specifications & Harness Pinouts

Disconnect electrical power before probing harness terminals. Set your digital multimeter to the lowest Ohms (Ω) scale and verify the circuit integrity against OEM specifications:

Component & Harness Terminals Expected OEM Reading (Normal) Failure Threshold (Replace Component)
Evaporator Fan DC Supply (Red to Black) 12.0V DC steady supply voltage 0.0V DC (Blown driver circuit)
Freezer Thermistor at 32°F 32.0 kΩ to 33.5 kΩ (Nominal sensor) Infinite (OL) open sensor
Condenser Fan Motor Winding 1.5 kΩ to 2.2 kΩ (Normal coil) Infinite (OL) open circuit

5. Step-by-Step Mechanical Tear-Down & Repair

Follow these 5 OEM service manual steps to access, inspect, and replace the failing component safely:

  1. Silence Audible Alarm: Press 'Alarm Reset' pad on dispenser panel.
  2. Remove Front Toe Grille: Snap off black plastic lower toe kick grille on front bottom.
  3. Vacuum Condenser Coils: Use a long flexible coil brush and vacuum cleaner crevice tool to pull thick lint carpets off the metal condenser coils.
  4. Verify Fan Rotation: Look through bottom grille with flashlight: confirm condenser fan blade next to compressor is spinning rapidly.
  5. Monitor Cabinet Temperature: Place digital thermometer in freezer: confirm temperature drops to 0°F over next 4 hours.

6. Frequently Asked Questions

Why does H1 display after loading groceries?

Loading a large volume of room-temperature groceries warms the freezer cavity temporarily. If temperature stays above 26°F for 60 minutes, the H1 alarm activates automatically as a reminder.

How long does it take for H1 to turn off?

Once the underlying cooling issue is resolved, the compressor must pull the freezer below 26°F before firmware will clear the H1 warning from the display.
