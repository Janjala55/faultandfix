Whirlpool Dishwasher Not Filling With Water: Inlet Valve W10872255 Diagnostic

By Fault and Fix Diagnostic Engineering Team
Updated: Nov 22, 2026
OEM Verified
Fault Code: No Water Entering Tub / Hums But Doesn't Fill Reviewed by Master Appliance Technician
What It Means: Water Fill Timeout Fault (Control board energizes fill solenoid for 90 seconds, but float switch or optical water indicator detects no water depth in basin)
Most Likely Root Cause: Hard water calcium deposits clogging the fine stainless filter screen of inlet valve (W10872255) or burned-out 120V solenoid actuator coil.
Repair Difficulty: Beginner to Intermediate DIY
Estimated Time: 25–35 minutes
Required Tools: 1/4" nut driver, channel lock pliers, digital multimeter, small bucket
Applicable Models: Whirlpool WDT710, WDT730, WDT750, WDTA50, KitchenAid, Maytag series
Immediate Safe Action / 60-Second Quick Check

Check overfill float in front left tub corner. Lift float up and down: it must click freely. If stuck in raised position, washer will never fill.

1. Symptoms & Operational Failure Signs

When code No Water Entering Tub / Hums But Doesn't Fill triggers, the appliance controller detects abnormal electrical feedback or physical boundary limits out of range. Check for these real-world failure symptoms:

  • Dishwasher starts, drain pump runs for 30s, but no rushing water sound follows.
  • Wash motor hums dry or cycle cancels after 3 minutes.
  • Dishes come out completely dry and detergent door never pops open.
  • Water inlet valve behind bottom kickplate hums or vibrates softly without admitting water.

2. The 30-Second Hard Reset Sequence

Clear temporary software glitches, parasitic capacitive voltage, and transient relay lockouts with this manufacturer capacitive discharge sequence:

  1. Open door and pour 1 gallon of warm tap water into bottom tub.
  2. Close door and press Start: if wash arms begin spraying, circulation motor is fine and inlet valve is bad.
  3. Turn off dishwasher circuit breaker for 3 minutes to reboot control logic.
  4. Verify under-sink hot water supply shutoff valve is fully open.

3. Entering Hidden Factory Diagnostic Mode

Execute this authorized technician button cadence to retrieve stored historical error logs and force individual component actuation tests:

  1. With console in standby, press any 3 buttons in order: 1-2-3, 1-2-3, 1-2-3.
  2. All lights illuminate in Service Diagnostic Mode.
  3. Step into interval 16 (Fill Valve Test).
  4. Listen for solenoid click and verify 120V AC is delivered to valve terminals.

Disconnect electrical power before probing harness terminals. Set your digital multimeter to the lowest Ohms (Ω) scale and verify the circuit integrity against OEM specifications:

5. Step-by-Step Mechanical Tear-Down & Repair

Follow these 5 OEM service manual steps to access, inspect, and replace the failing component safely:

  1. Disconnect Power & Water: Turn off circuit breaker and shut off hot water valve under sink.
  2. Remove Lower Kickplates: Use 1/4" or 5/16" nut driver to remove two screws holding lower toe panels.
  3. Locate Inlet Valve: Locate the brass/plastic water inlet valve mounted on the front left frame bracket.
  4. Disconnect Water Supply: Place a shallow pan under valve, unthread brass 3/8" water line fitting, and remove clamp on rubber outlet hose.
  5. Install OEM Replacement: Disconnect wiring plug, unscrew mounting bracket screw, install new OEM valve (W10872255), reconnect hoses, tighten compression fitting, and test for leaks.

6. Frequently Asked Questions

Can I just clean the sediment screen instead of buying a new valve?

You can clean loose sediment with a toothbrush. However, if the solenoid coil has begun to degrade or calcium scale has seized the internal plastic diaphragm, cleaning the screen will not fix the fill problem.

Why did the float switch stop the water from filling?

The mushroom-shaped plastic float in the front corner is a mechanical flood safeguard. If grease or foreign debris wedges under the float stem, the switch remains open, locking out the water valve permanently.
