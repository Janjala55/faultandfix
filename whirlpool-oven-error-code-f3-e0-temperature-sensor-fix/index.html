Whirlpool Oven F3 E0 Error Code: Temperature Sensor RTD Diagnostic

By Fault and Fix Diagnostic Engineering Team
Updated: Nov 20, 2026
Reviewed by Marcus Vance (Senior Appliance Specialist)
5 min read
Verified OEM Fault Diagnostic F3 E0
Verified against Whirlpool OEM Engineering Service Specs
Official Fault Meaning: Oven Temperature Sensor (RTD Probe) Circuit Open or Above Maximum Limit
Most Likely Root Cause: Failed platinum RTD temperature sensor probe inside rear oven cavity, broken sensor lead, or disconnected sensor plug.
Repair Difficulty: Intermediate DIY
Estimated Time: 20 mins
Required Tools: 1/4-inch Nut Driver, Digital Multimeter, Flashlight
Applicable Models: WFE515S0ES, WFE505W0HZ, WFG505M0BS, WEE745H0FS, WEE510S0FS, WGE745C0FS
Immediate Safe Action / 60-Second Quick Check

Turn off breaker. Open oven door and inspect the pencil-thin metal sensor probe at the top left/right rear corner.

1. Symptoms & Operational Failure Signs

When code F3 E0 triggers, the central microcontroller detects anomalous feedback or sensor voltage out of range. Check for these symptoms:

  • 'F3 E0' flashing on clock display; oven sounds continuous error beeps
  • Bake and broil heating elements or gas burners refuse to ignite
  • Oven overheats uncontrollably or incinerates food during baking
  • Display shows 'ERR' when attempting to set temperature setpoint

2. The 30-Second Hard Reset Sequence

Clear temporary software lockouts, residual filter capacitor voltage, and transient logic anomalies with this factory capacitive drain routine:

  1. Turn off the range 40A/50A 240V circuit breaker at the electrical breaker box.
  2. Wait 10 minutes to allow the electronic clock control (ERC) microcomputer to reset.
  3. Inspect the metal sensor probe in the top rear corner of the oven interior for damage.
  4. Restore breaker power. If probe resistance is normal, the F3 E0 alarm clears.

3. Entering Hidden Factory Diagnostic Mode

Execute this technician keypad/dial cadence to force direct component test routines, cycle individual actuators, and read stored historical fault codes:

  1. Disconnect electrical power.
  2. Access the 2-pin sensor harness connector (either by unscrewing sensor from inside oven or behind rear sheet metal panel).
  3. Set digital multimeter to the 2,000 Ohm scale.
  4. Measure resistance across the two sensor terminal pins at room temperature (77°F). It must read between 1,050 and 1,100 Ohms.

4. Multimeter Resistance (Ω) Specifications & Harness Pinouts

Disconnect electrical power before probing harness terminals. Set your digital multimeter to the lowest Ohms (Ω) scale and test the critical circuit path:

5. Step-by-Step Mechanical Tear-Down & Repair

Follow these 5 OEM service manual steps to access, inspect, and replace the failing component safely:

  1. Power Off: Trip the double-pole 40A/50A range circuit breaker before touching wiring.
  2. Oven Cavity Access: Open oven door. Locate the pencil-thin metal sensor probe at the upper rear corner.
  3. Probe Screws Removal: Remove the two 1/4-inch hex screws securing the probe mounting flange to the back wall.
  4. Harness Extraction: Gently pull probe and wire leads through the hole until the quick-disconnect molex plug appears.
  5. Sensor Replacement: Unclip old sensor, plug in new OEM temperature sensor (part W10181986 / WPW10181986), screw flange back, and test.

6. Frequently Asked Questions

What is the standard resistance for a Whirlpool oven sensor?

Whirlpool uses a precision platinum RTD (Resistance Temperature Detector) sensor that measures exactly 1,080 Ohms at room temperature (77°F / 25°C). For every 1°F rise in temperature, resistance increases by approximately 2 Ohms.

Can I replace the sensor from inside the oven without moving the range?

Yes! On nearly all modern Whirlpool ranges, the wire harness connector is designed to pull forward 2 to 3 inches through the cavity hole, allowing complete replacement from the front without sliding the stove out.
