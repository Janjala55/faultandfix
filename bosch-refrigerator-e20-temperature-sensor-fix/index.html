Bosch Refrigerator E20 Error Code: Fridge Compartment Thermistor 00619216 Diagnostic

By Fault and Fix Diagnostic Engineering Team
Updated: Dec 02, 2026
OEM Verified
Fault Code: E20 (Fresh Food Temperature Sensor Circuit Failure) Reviewed by Master Appliance Technician
What It Means: Refrigerator Compartment Thermistor Open or Shorted (Main control board detects thermistor resistance out of normal operating range of 1.0 kΩ to 50 kΩ)
Most Likely Root Cause: Fractured NTC thermistor bead inside plastic sensor capsule (00619216 / 00614488) or severed wire harness behind interior liner.
Repair Difficulty: Intermediate DIY
Estimated Time: 30–40 minutes
Required Tools: Torx T20 screwdriver, digital multimeter, heat shrink tubing, wire strippers
Applicable Models: Bosch 500, 800 Series, B36CL80, B36CT80, French Door and Bottom Mount series
Immediate Safe Action / 60-Second Quick Check

Press 'Alarm Off' pad to silence beeping. Code E20 indicates fresh food temperature sensor is disconnected or broken.

1. Symptoms & Operational Failure Signs

When code E20 (Fresh Food Temperature Sensor Circuit Failure) triggers, the appliance controller detects abnormal electrical feedback or physical boundary limits out of range. Check for these real-world failure symptoms:

  • Display flashes 'E20' continuously accompanied by alarm chime.
  • Fresh food section freezes food solid (down to 25°F) or warms up to 55°F.
  • Compressor runs constantly or fails to cycle off.
  • Temperature digits disappear from front display panel.

2. The 30-Second Hard Reset Sequence

Clear temporary software glitches, parasitic capacitive voltage, and transient relay lockouts with this manufacturer capacitive discharge sequence:

  1. Unplug refrigerator power cord for 5 minutes.
  2. Open fresh food doors and inspect rear sensor grille on right wall.
  3. Restore power: if E20 reappears within 30 seconds, sensor replacement is necessary.
  4. Reboot clears temporary firmware logic glitch.

3. Entering Hidden Factory Diagnostic Mode

Execute this authorized technician button cadence to retrieve stored historical error logs and force individual component actuation tests:

  1. Enter Customer Service Mode: Hold 'Super' and 'Alarm' pads for 5 seconds.
  2. Advance to Step P01 (Sensor Readout).
  3. Display shows real-time fresh food sensor reading in Celsius.
  4. If display reads '-99' or '99', the sensor is open or shorted.

4. Multimeter Resistance (Ω) Specifications & Harness Pinouts

Disconnect electrical power before probing harness terminals. Set your digital multimeter to the lowest Ohms (Ω) scale and verify the circuit integrity against OEM specifications:

Component & Harness Terminals Expected OEM Reading (Normal) Failure Threshold (Replace Component)
Fresh Food Thermistor at 39°F (4°C) 14.5 kΩ to 16.0 kΩ (Negative Temp Coefficient) Infinite (OL) open circuit or 0 Ω short
Thermistor at 77°F (25°C) 4.8 kΩ to 5.2 kΩ (5 kΩ nominal) Unchanging static resistance
Evaporator Fan DC Supply 12.0V DC steady output 0.0V DC (Blown driver transistor)

5. Step-by-Step Mechanical Tear-Down & Repair

Follow these 5 OEM service manual steps to access, inspect, and replace the failing component safely:

  1. Disconnect Electrical Supply: Unplug refrigerator power cord.
  2. Locate Sensor Housing: Inside fresh food section, locate small plastic vented sensor cover on middle right wall.
  3. Unclip Sensor Cover: Unscrew Torx T20 screw and pry off plastic cover.
  4. Cut & Splice Sensor Wires: Cut sensor wire leads 2 inches from old sensor bead. Strip insulation 1/4-inch.
  5. Install OEM Replacement: Splice new OEM sensor (00619216) using waterproof gel crimp connectors and heat shrink tubing, snap into cover, and restore power.

6. Frequently Asked Questions

Why did my vegetables freeze when error E20 showed?

When the control board loses communication with the temperature sensor, it enters failsafe emergency cooling to prevent food spoilage, running the compressor 100% of the time and freezing fresh food solid.

Do I have to pull the refrigerator out to replace this sensor?

No! The fresh food thermistor is completely serviced from inside the refrigerator compartment without pulling the heavy unit away from the wall.
