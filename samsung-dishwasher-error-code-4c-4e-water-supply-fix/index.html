Samsung Dishwasher 4C / 4E Error Code: Water Fill Valve & Flow Diagnostic

By Fault and Fix Diagnostic Engineering Team
Updated: Nov 10, 2026
Reviewed by Marcus Vance (Senior Appliance Specialist)
5 min read
Verified OEM Fault Diagnostic 4C / 4E
Verified against Samsung OEM Engineering Service Specs
Official Fault Meaning: Water Supply Low Flow Timeout (Dishwasher flow meter fails to detect minimum required water volume within 6 minutes of valve opening)
Most Likely Root Cause: Sediment-clogged valve inlet screen, closed sink supply shutoff valve, or burned water valve solenoid coil (DD62-00084A).
Repair Difficulty: Intermediate DIY
Estimated Time: 20–30 minutes
Required Tools: Channel-lock pliers, digital multimeter, 1/4" nut driver, small bucket
Applicable Models: Samsung DW80R9950, DW80K7050, DW80M9960, DW80R2031 series
Immediate Safe Action / 60-Second Quick Check

Check under-sink water supply valve to verify the 3/8" braided steel feed line is turned 100% counter-clockwise.

1. Symptoms & Operational Failure Signs

When code 4C / 4E triggers, the central microcontroller detects anomalous feedback or sensor voltage out of range. Check for these symptoms:

  • Dishwasher hums at start of cycle but no water sprays or splashes inside tub
  • Cycle runs for 4 to 6 minutes before aborting with flashing '4C' or '4E' error chime
  • Tub water level does not reach the bottom heating element or sump filter ring
  • Fill valve makes vibrating buzz but water barely trickles into side water wall chamber

2. The 30-Second Hard Reset Sequence

Clear temporary software lockouts, residual filter capacitor voltage, and transient logic anomalies with this factory capacitive drain routine:

  1. Press Power to turn unit off. Turn off water supply stop valve under kitchen sink.
  2. Unplug dishwasher or switch off circuit breaker for 30 seconds to clear controller timer.
  3. Disconnect braided water supply line from valve inlet elbow, clean screen, and reconnect.
  4. Turn water supply back on, restore power, and run 'Quick 60' cycle to confirm fill.

3. Entering Hidden Factory Diagnostic Mode

Execute this technician keypad/dial cadence to force direct component test routines, cycle individual actuators, and read stored historical fault codes:

  1. Enter diagnostic service mode: Press 'Normal' + 'Heavy' + 'Power'.
  2. Advance test mode to Step 01 (Water Inlet Valve Test).
  3. Listen for valve snap and observe flow meter pulse rate on digital display.
  4. Flow meter should report continuous pulse count (approx. 200 pulses per liter of water delivered).

4. Multimeter Resistance (Ω) Specifications & Harness Pinouts

Disconnect electrical power before probing harness terminals. Set your digital multimeter to the lowest Ohms (Ω) scale and test the critical circuit path:

5. Step-by-Step Mechanical Tear-Down & Repair

Follow these 5 OEM service manual steps to access, inspect, and replace the failing component safely:

  1. Safety & Water Shutoff: Switch off breaker and turn off water supply valve under kitchen sink.
  2. Toe-Kick Access: Remove 2 Phillips screws securing bottom front toe-kick plate and pull insulation pad away.
  3. Valve Inspection: Locate water inlet valve mounted to lower left frame bracket. Inspect brass 3/8" compression fitting for sediment.
  4. Screen Cleaning: Disconnect braided water supply line. Use needle-nose pliers to remove fine mesh filter screen from brass valve inlet. Rinse with vinegar.
  5. Valve Replacement: If screen is clean but solenoid tests open circuit OL, disconnect 2-pin harness, unscrew mounting bracket, install new OEM valve (DD62-00084A), and test for leaks.

6. Frequently Asked Questions

Can low home water pressure trigger dishwasher error 4C?

Yes! Samsung dishwashers require a minimum dynamic water pressure of 20 PSI (140 kPa). If residential water pressure drops below this threshold or a flood-safe hose trips, the 6-minute fill timer will expire before sufficient water is logged.

What is the flow meter and where is it located?

The flow meter is a small plastic wheel containing a tiny magnet mounted inside the left-side water inlet air break chamber. As water rushes past, the wheel spins over a magnetic reed switch, sending pulses to the computer to meter precise volume.
