Samsung Dryer Shuts Off After 5 Minutes on Auto-Sensor: Sensor Bars DC61-02627A Diagnostic

By Fault and Fix Diagnostic Engineering Team
Updated: Dec 01, 2026
OEM Verified
Fault Code: Shuts Off After 5–10 Minutes / Clothes Still Wet Reviewed by Master Appliance Technician
What It Means: False Dry Detection / Sensor Bars Insulated (Control board detects high electrical resistance across moisture bars, concluding clothes are 100% dry and entering cooling phase)
Most Likely Root Cause: Wax and fabric softener residue from dryer sheets coating the curved stainless steel moisture sensor bars (DC61-02627A), insulating metal contacts from damp laundry.
Repair Difficulty: Beginner DIY
Estimated Time: 15–20 minutes
Required Tools: Fine steel wool (0000) or Scotch-Brite pad, rubbing alcohol, microfiber cloth
Applicable Models: Samsung DVE45R6100, DVE50T5300, DVG50R5400, DV42H5000 series
Immediate Safe Action / 60-Second Quick Check

Switch cycle to 'Time Dry' for 40 minutes on Medium Heat. If dryer runs full 40 minutes and dries clothes, your moisture sensor bars are 100% coated in wax.

1. Symptoms & Operational Failure Signs

When code Shuts Off After 5–10 Minutes / Clothes Still Wet triggers, the appliance controller detects abnormal electrical feedback or physical boundary limits out of range. Check for these real-world failure symptoms:

  • Dryer stops after 5 to 10 minutes on Sensor Dry cycle and plays end-of-cycle chime.
  • Clothes inside drum are still cold, damp, or completely wet.
  • Dryer works normally on Time Dry or Quick Dry cycles.
  • Moisture sensor bars appear dull, cloudy, or yellowed.

2. The 30-Second Hard Reset Sequence

Clear temporary software glitches, parasitic capacitive voltage, and transient relay lockouts with this manufacturer capacitive discharge sequence:

  1. Disconnect dryer power cord from 120V/240V outlet.
  2. Locate the two curved metal sensor strips mounted in front lint filter housing.
  3. Scrub both metal bars vigorously with fine steel wool and 90%+ isopropyl alcohol.
  4. Wipe residue clean with damp cloth and run Sensor Dry test.

3. Entering Hidden Factory Diagnostic Mode

Execute this authorized technician button cadence to retrieve stored historical error logs and force individual component actuation tests:

  1. Enter Diagnostics: Press 'Time' and 'Dry Level' for 7 seconds until chime sounds.
  2. Rotate selector knob to Sensor Dry Test (SE).
  3. Bridge both metal sensor bars with a damp wet washcloth.
  4. Display must beep and toggle digits, confirming moisture detection.

4. Multimeter Resistance (Ω) Specifications & Harness Pinouts

Disconnect electrical power before probing harness terminals. Set your digital multimeter to the lowest Ohms (Ω) scale and verify the circuit integrity against OEM specifications:

Component & Harness Terminals Expected OEM Reading (Normal) Failure Threshold (Replace Component)
Moisture Sensor Resistance (Open/Dry) Infinite (OL) open circuit across bars Under 1 MΩ (Moisture bridge across dry housing)
Sensor Resistance (Bridged with Wet Finger) 50 kΩ to 200 kΩ (Conductive circuit) Infinite (OL) broken sensor wire harness
Exhaust Thermistor Resistance at 77°F 9.5 kΩ to 11.0 kΩ (10 kΩ nominal) Infinite (OL) open circuit

5. Step-by-Step Mechanical Tear-Down & Repair

Follow these 5 OEM service manual steps to access, inspect, and replace the failing component safely:

  1. Disconnect Electrical Supply: Unplug dryer power cord.
  2. Locate Sensor Strips: Open dryer door. Below the lint screen opening, locate two curved stainless steel strips embedded in grey plastic grill.
  3. Scrub Wax Coating: Use a fine Scotch-Brite pad or 0000 steel wool soaked in rubbing alcohol to scrub the metal surfaces.
  4. Inspect Wire Connections: Remove lower grill mounting screws to verify sensor spade connectors have not slipped off rear terminals.
  5. Test with Damp Laundry: Reassemble grill, plug dryer in, select Normal Sensor Dry, and verify cycle runs full duration.

6. Frequently Asked Questions

Why do dryer sheets ruin moisture sensor bars?

Fabric softener sheets contain animal fat and silicone wax designed to melt onto clothing under heat. This same wax deposits onto the metal sensor bars, creating an invisible insulating shield that blocks electrical conductivity.

How often should I clean the sensor bars?

If you use fabric softener dryer sheets regularly, scrub the moisture sensor bars with rubbing alcohol once every 4 to 6 months to maintain accurate drying times.
