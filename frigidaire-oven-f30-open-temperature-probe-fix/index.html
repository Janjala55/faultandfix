Frigidaire Oven F30 Error Code: Open Temperature Sensor Probe 316217002 Diagnostic

By Fault and Fix Diagnostic Engineering Team
Updated: Nov 27, 2026
OEM Verified
Fault Code: F30 (Open Oven Temperature Sensor Circuit) Reviewed by Master Appliance Technician
What It Means: Oven Temperature Sensor Circuit Open (Electronic oven control board detects infinite resistance on RTD probe input terminals, indicating severed wire or bad probe)
Most Likely Root Cause: Fractured platinum RTD sensor element inside probe (316217002) or disconnected 2-pin Molex harness connector behind upper rear cabinet panel.
Repair Difficulty: Intermediate DIY
Estimated Time: 25–35 minutes
Required Tools: 1/4" nut driver, digital multimeter, work gloves
Applicable Models: Frigidaire Gallery, Professional, FFEF3054, FFGF3054, Electrolux Range series
Immediate Safe Action / 60-Second Quick Check

Press Cancel/Clear to silence beeper. Code F30 will remain locked on screen until a valid 1,080 ohm sensor resistance is reconnected to the control board.

1. Symptoms & Operational Failure Signs

When code F30 (Open Oven Temperature Sensor Circuit) triggers, the appliance controller detects abnormal electrical feedback or physical boundary limits out of range. Check for these real-world failure symptoms:

  • Oven emits rapid continuous alarm chimes and displays 'F30'.
  • Bake and Broil functions are completely disabled and refuse to start.
  • Oven cancelled baking mid-cycle with sudden beep.
  • Error F30 appears immediately upon powering range on.

2. The 30-Second Hard Reset Sequence

Clear temporary software glitches, parasitic capacitive voltage, and transient relay lockouts with this manufacturer capacitive discharge sequence:

  1. Turn off range 240V/120V circuit breaker at main service panel.
  2. Wait 5 minutes to clear temporary microprocessor lockout memory.
  3. Restore breaker power.
  4. If F30 beeps again within 10 seconds, the RTD sensor circuit is physically open.

3. Entering Hidden Factory Diagnostic Mode

Execute this authorized technician button cadence to retrieve stored historical error logs and force individual component actuation tests:

  1. Turn off power. Pull range forward to access rear sheet metal.
  2. Locate 2-wire white connector for temperature probe in upper rear corner.
  3. Unplug connector and measure resistance across 2 sensor pins with multimeter.
  4. Must read 1,050 Ω to 1,100 Ω at room temperature. If Infinite OL, probe is dead.

4. Multimeter Resistance (Ω) Specifications & Harness Pinouts

Disconnect electrical power before probing harness terminals. Set your digital multimeter to the lowest Ohms (Ω) scale and verify the circuit integrity against OEM specifications:

5. Step-by-Step Mechanical Tear-Down & Repair

Follow these 5 OEM service manual steps to access, inspect, and replace the failing component safely:

  1. Disconnect 240V Power: Turn off range breakers in service panel.
  2. Remove Upper Rear Cover: Remove 1/4" hex screws holding rear upper sheet metal console cover.
  3. Unplug Probe Connector: Locate 2-pin Molex connector for temperature probe in upper corner. Disconnect plug.
  4. Unbolt Probe Inside Oven: Open oven door, locate metal sensor probe in upper left rear corner. Remove two 1/4" hex screws holding probe flange.
  5. Install OEM Replacement: Pull old probe into oven, feed new OEM probe (316217002) harness through back wall, tighten mounting screws, plug in connector, and test.

6. Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between error F10 and F30 on Frigidaire ovens?

F10 indicates runaway high temperature (or shorted sensor), while F30 specifically indicates an Open Sensor Circuit (infinite resistance from severed wires or broken RTD element).

Can I test the probe from the front of the oven without pulling it out?

Yes! Remove the two mounting screws holding the probe inside the oven cavity and gently pull the wire harness 3 inches into the oven to access the connector plug for multimeter testing.
