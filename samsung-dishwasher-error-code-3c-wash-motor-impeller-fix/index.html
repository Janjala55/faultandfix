Samsung Dishwasher 3C Error Code: Wash Motor Impeller Jam & Vane Diagnostic

By Fault and Fix Diagnostic Engineering Team
Updated: Nov 10, 2026
Reviewed by Marcus Vance (Senior Appliance Specialist)
5 min read
Verified OEM Fault Diagnostic 3C
Verified against Samsung OEM Engineering Service Specs
Official Fault Meaning: Circulation Wash Pump Motor Fault (Inverter detects high motor current, seized impeller, or open 3-phase motor winding)
Most Likely Root Cause: Broken glass shard or toothpicks jamming wash impeller vane, or burned BLDC pump motor windings (DD31-00013B).
Repair Difficulty: Intermediate DIY
Estimated Time: 35–50 minutes
Required Tools: Torx T20 screwdriver, digital multimeter, 1/4" nut driver, needle-nose pliers
Applicable Models: Samsung DW80R9950, DW80K7050, DW80M9960, DW80J3020 series
Immediate Safe Action / 60-Second Quick Check

Remove bottom dish rack, unscrew sump filter cylinder, and reach down into sump intake to feel for jammed foreign objects.

1. Symptoms & Operational Failure Signs

When code 3C triggers, the central microcontroller detects anomalous feedback or sensor voltage out of range. Check for these symptoms:

  • Dishwasher fills with water normally but spray arms never spin or spray
  • Quiet buzzing or groaning sound from bottom sump for 15 seconds followed by error chime
  • Display flashes '3C' and machine aborts cycle by draining tub water
  • Dishes remain dirty and detergent tablet drops into bottom tub undissolved

2. The 30-Second Hard Reset Sequence

Clear temporary software lockouts, residual filter capacitor voltage, and transient logic anomalies with this factory capacitive drain routine:

  1. Press Power to turn unit off. Shut off dishwasher circuit breaker for 60 seconds.
  2. Remove bottom rack, extract stainless sump filter, and clean all debris.
  3. Restore breaker power, start 'Express 60' cycle, and listen for wash motor spin-up.
  4. If groaning sound returns without spray pressure, motor is mechanically jammed.

3. Entering Hidden Factory Diagnostic Mode

Execute this technician keypad/dial cadence to force direct component test routines, cycle individual actuators, and read stored historical fault codes:

  1. Enter technician diagnostic: Hold 'Normal' + 'Heavy' + 'Power' for 3 seconds.
  2. Press 'Delay Start' button to toggle manual actuator step test to Step 04 (Circulation Pump).
  3. Observe pump operation: controller will attempt to ramp motor to 2,800 RPM.
  4. If error 3C trips immediately, measure winding phase resistance on pump motor harness.

4. Multimeter Resistance (Ω) Specifications & Harness Pinouts

Disconnect electrical power before probing harness terminals. Set your digital multimeter to the lowest Ohms (Ω) scale and test the critical circuit path:

Component & Harness Terminals Expected OEM Reading (Normal) Failure Threshold (Replace Component)
BLDC Wash Motor Phase Windings (U-V, V-W, W-U)
3-Pin Motor Connector (DD31-00013B)		 12.5 Ω to 15.5 Ω (Balanced across all 3 phases) Infinite OL (Burned coil) or 0.0 Ω (Shorted phase)
Motor Phase-to-Chassis Ground
Any motor terminal to metal chassis ground		 Infinite OL (> 50 MΩ) < 1.0 MΩ (Short to ground / Water ingress)
Inverter DC Supply Rail
Main PCB to Inverter Board Harness		 12V DC logic rail and 310V DC bus rail 0V DC (Blown onboard rectifier/fuse)

5. Step-by-Step Mechanical Tear-Down & Repair

Follow these 5 OEM service manual steps to access, inspect, and replace the failing component safely:

  1. Unit Extraction: Switch off breaker and turn off water supply under sink. Disconnect drain hose and pull dishwasher 6 inches forward from cabinet cavity.
  2. Access Lower Sump: Lay dishwasher gently on its back. Remove bottom metal pan access screws (disconnect leak sensor harness).
  3. Motor Location: Locate large cylindrical circulation wash pump attached to main plastic sump bowl with rubber vibration isolators.
  4. Impeller Inspection: Twist circulation motor 1/4 turn counter-clockwise to detach from sump bayonet mount. Inspect impeller vanes for jammed glass or bone chips.
  5. Motor Replacement: If impeller spins freely but motor coils test open, install new OEM circulation pump (DD31-00013B), lubricate O-ring seal, and reassemble.

6. Frequently Asked Questions

Can hard food debris cause error 3C?

Yes! Broken drinking glass fragments, lemon seeds, and toothpicks frequently bypass the coarse filter screen, lodging directly between the high-speed centrifugal impeller blades and the plastic pump volute.

Why does error 3C drain water out before quitting?

Samsung dishwashers are programmed to dump the water charge whenever the wash pump fails to circulate within 40 seconds to prevent standing soapy water from stagnating or leaking.
